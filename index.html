HOW IT WORKS

Your Dubai Flying Dress Photoshoot

Three simple steps. One unforgettable experience.

From choosing your dress to receiving your final gallery, we make your Dubai
flying dress photoshoot simple, comfortable and completely organized.

STEP 01

Choose Your Flying
Dress & Location

Select your favourite flying dress and choose your photoshoot setting — from the golden Dubai desert to iconic city locations. Message us on WhatsApp and our team will help you check availability, choose your dress and find the best shooting time.

STEP 02

Enjoy Your Flying
Dress Photoshoot

Meet your professional photographer and female assistant at your selected location. We'll guide you through posing, dress movements and different angles while creating the signature flying dress effect that makes every image stand out.

STEP 03

Receive Your Edited
Photos & Video

After your session, receive your professionally retouched images through a private online gallery. Selected packages also include vertical video content perfect for Instagram Reels, TikTok and sharing your Dubai experience.
